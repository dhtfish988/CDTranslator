CD翻译 隐私政策

最后更新日期：2026年1月21日

概述

CD翻译（以下简称"本应用"）非常重视用户的隐私保护。本隐私政策说明了本应用如何收集、使用和保护您的信息。

信息收集

本应用不会收集、存储或传输您的任何个人信息。具体而言：

  • 翻译文本：您输入的翻译文本会通过第三方翻译服务（Google翻译）进行处理，本应用不会存储这些文本。
  • 图片识别：图片文字识别完全在本地设备上进行，使用 macOS 系统自带的 Vision 框架，图片数据不会上传到任何服务器。
  • 剪贴板数据：本应用可能会读取剪贴板中的图片以进行文字识别，但不会存储或传输剪贴板内容。

第三方服务

本应用使用以下第三方服务进行翻译：

  • Google翻译：翻译请求会发送到 Google 服务器。请参阅 Google 隐私政策了解更多信息。

数据安全

本应用采用以下措施保护您的数据：

  • 所有网络请求均使用 HTTPS 加密传输
  • 图片识别完全在本地进行，不上传任何图片数据
  • 本应用运行在 macOS 沙箱环境中，限制了对系统资源的访问

用户权利

由于本应用不收集或存储任何个人数据，因此不存在需要删除或导出的用户数据。

儿童隐私

本应用不专门针对13岁以下的儿童，也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

隐私政策更新

我们可能会不时更新本隐私政策。更新后的政策将在本页面发布，并更新"最后更新日期"。

如果您对本隐私政策有任何疑问，请通过以下方式联系我们：

电子邮件：support@cdtranslate.app

CD Translate Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 21, 2026

Overview

CD Translate ("the App") takes user privacy very seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how the App collects, uses, and protects your information.

Information Collection

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. Specifically:

  • Translation Text: Text you enter for translation is processed through third-party translation services (Google Translate). The App does not store this text.
  • Image Recognition: Image text recognition is performed entirely on your local device using macOS's built-in Vision framework. Image data is never uploaded to any server.
  • Clipboard Data: The App may read images from your clipboard for text recognition but does not store or transmit clipboard contents.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services for translation:

  • Google Translate: Translation requests are sent to Google servers. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for more information.

Data Security

The App employs the following measures to protect your data:

  • All network requests use HTTPS encryption
  • Image recognition is performed locally without uploading any image data
  • The App runs in the macOS sandbox environment, limiting access to system resources

User Rights

Since the App does not collect or store any personal data, there is no user data to delete or export.

Children's Privacy

The App is not specifically targeted at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated policies will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
